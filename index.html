Group rotation

How it works

  1. Use the groups shown below at your next get-together.
  2. Someone can't make it? Open that box under the groups and tap their name. The groups re-deal without them, for that round only.
  3. Afterwards, tap the blue "Round is done" button. The next round appears.

Repeat until everyone has been in a group with everyone else. Everything saves by itself, and Undo at the top takes back your last change. The moon or sun button switches between dark and light.
